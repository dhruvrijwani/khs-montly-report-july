💡 Campaign Structure & Creative Performance

April was a strong month driven by the Summer Sale in the first half. Campaigns across Meta and Google were built around sale-specific creatives — statics, carousels, and videos — with strong support from catalog ads in the Wallpaper and Ewall categories. Retargeting, Wallpaper, and Wall Art campaigns on Meta were the top performers across the full month. From April 16th onwards, BAU campaigns were resumed across Meta and Google with budgets shifted accordingly to sustain momentum through the non-sale period.

🚀 Next Steps – May 2026

May Strategic Focus

Building on April's strong sale performance, May shifts focus to sustaining BAU momentum and activating retention channels:

  • Continue BAU Campaigns on Meta & Google: Keep performance campaigns live across both platforms, maintaining the catalog and retargeting structures that drove strong results in April's non-sale period. Optimise budgets based on daily performance signals.
  • Setup Email & WhatsApp Flows for Retention: Activate automated retention workflows using Klaviyo for email and Delight Chat for WhatsApp. Focus on post-purchase flows, browse abandonment, and re-engagement sequences to convert existing customers and recover lost intent.
