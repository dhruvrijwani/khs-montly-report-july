01 · Executive Summary

Top Performance Overview

India returned 6.12x on the online store, and Global grew on September.

₹85.47L in India all-channel sales, with online store net ROAS at 6.12x.

October 2026 delivered ₹85.47L India all-channel sales across 710 orders, with the online store contributing ₹52.14L ex-GST from 606 orders on a spend of ₹8,51,789, a 6.12x return on the net figure. Spend was within 2.4% of September, while sales were lower than a September that carried a 15-day Festive Season Sale. October's Homecoming Sale ran for six days, 12 to 17 October, at flat 25% off.

The Global store reached ₹24.28L all-channel across 42 orders, with ₹17.91L net on a spend of ₹2,68,114, a 6.68x return on the net figure and an all-channel AOV of ₹57,801. On GA4, Google returned 6.86x against 0.91x for Meta in India (Section 03).

🛍️

India stepped back from a sale-heavy September

All-channel sales were 25.2% lower and online store ex-GST 31.1% lower than September, on 2.4% more spend. Net ROAS went from 9.11x to 6.12x. September ran a 7 to 21 September sale, so this is not a like-for-like comparison.

🌍

Global grew on September

Global all-channel sales rose 19.8% (₹20.27L to ₹24.28L) on 64.8% more spend, with orders from 38 to 42 and net ROAS from 8.16x to 6.68x. The order base is small (37 online store orders), so a few high-value orders move these figures.

🔎

Google led on GA4 in India

Google Search and PMax were credited with 75.9% of GA4 revenue on 29.6% of spend in the India campaign table. Google Global spent ₹64,585 for 1 platform-reported order.

Pre-sale and sale-live WhatsApp sends led

The 13 October sale send was credited with ₹3,50,000 from 8 orders and the 10 October pre-sale send with ₹3,10,000 from 20 orders. The 10 October pre-sale send drove the most orders of any campaign, and the 17 October reminder followed with 15.

📣
WhatsApp: the last sends reached fewer people

The 16 and 17 October broadcasts reached only 55% and 51% of their reachable audience, and the 12 and 14 October sends had 4.2K and 5.9K failed. Understanding why sends stop short is the first check before adding volume for the next sale.

✉️
Email: wide reach, thin engagement

Across the 8 email sends 78.3% of messages were delivered and 3.0% of delivered emails were read. India sends were credited with ₹3,03,000 from 54 orders, while Global and Creative Club email had none.

How to read retention revenue

The platform credits an order to a message when the customer was delivered it within 7 days before buying. This is attribution, not proven incremental revenue: a customer who was messaged and would have bought anyway during the sale is still counted, and one order can be credited to more than one campaign. WhatsApp and email figures are therefore shown separately and are not added together. Only campaigns with figures are shown, and paused or empty campaigns are left out. Counts and revenue are as displayed in the platform (K and L rounded), so totals are approximate, and two WhatsApp campaigns were still retrying when the data was taken. Two entries show ₹1 of revenue for one order, which looks like a tracking placeholder.

07 · What We Learned

Key Takeaways This Month

A read across performance, products, channels, creatives and retention.
01 · PERFORMANCE

India held a 6.12x net return, and Global grew

India online store ex-GST was ₹52.14L at a net ROAS of 6.12x (September 9.11x), on spend within 2.4% of September. Global all-channel sales rose 19.8% to ₹24.28L, with net ROAS at 6.68x on 64.8% more spend. Global has a small order base (37 online store orders), so a few large orders move it.

02 · CONTEXT

A shorter sale month, and the next step is the non-sale days

September carried a 15-day Festive Season Sale, while October's Homecoming Sale ran for six days (12 to 17 October), so the two months are not like-for-like. The aim for November is to grow across both the sale and the non-sale periods.

03 · PRODUCTS

Wallpaper leads revenue, Sample leads volume

Wallpaper was 44.2% of item revenue (51.7% in September) but 7.9% of items purchased. Embroidered Wall Art, Custom and Standard together, was 18.5% of revenue, and Sample was the biggest line by volume at 19.3%. "(not set)" is 13.3% of revenue and 17.0% of items, so fixing item tagging in GA4 will make the product view more reliable.

04 · CHANNELS

Google led on GA4 in India

In India, Google returned 6.86x on GA4 against 0.91x for Meta, while platform-reported ROAS was 5.82x for Google and 6.40x for Meta. The gap on Meta is worth understanding, for example tracking and attribution windows, before budget moves. Google Global spent ₹64,585 for 1 order and needs a review. Allocation continues to follow the weekly GA4 report.

05 · CREATIVES

Catalogue and E-Wall video ads led on return

The Ewall catalogue (15x), E-Wall video (12x) and Canvas Art catalogue (10x) ranked highest. The wallpaper catalogue, Last 90D and wallpaper video ads drove 127 of the 221 orders across the 8 ads shown, and the wallpaper video returned 5x on ₹58,943, the most video spend. ROAS here is rounded to whole numbers.

06 · RETENTION

Pre-sale and sale-live WhatsApp sends led

The 13 October WhatsApp send was credited with ₹3,50,000 and the 10 October pre-sale send with ₹3,10,000 from 20 orders. India email was credited with ₹3,03,000 from 54 orders, led by the 17 October last-day reminder. The 16 and 17 October WhatsApp broadcasts reached about half of their reachable audience, which is worth checking before the next sale. All figures are delivery-based, not incremental.
