01 · Executive Summary

Top Performance Overview

A month of new creative testing and Trade Program launches, with Google leading on GA4-attributed efficiency.

₹50.90L in India all-channel sales, with Global scaling to ₹8.59L at a 5.09x return.

August 2026 delivered ₹50.90L India all-channel sales across 495 orders, with the online store contributing ₹32.23L ex-GST from 427 orders on a spend of ₹6,52,964, a healthy 4.94x return on the net figure. 07 · What We Learned

Key Takeaways This Month

An honest read across products, channels, retention and the funnel, and what we will do about it.
Products

Wallpaper carries the business, samples open the door

Wallpaper is 66.9% of revenue but only 12.5% of units sold, so it is clearly our high-value core. By units, samples (29.1%), upholstery and cushions lead, which tells us lower-ticket items are how new customers first try the brand before committing to a full wallpaper order. Next: keep pushing new arrivals and use samples deliberately as the first step into higher-value purchases, which is what the festive high-AOV catalogue tests are built around.

Performance

Google is the efficient engine, Meta needs rebalancing

On GA4, Google returned 4.68x versus Meta's 0.61x, yet Meta carried the larger spend share this month (66%). The attributed sales are coming from Google Search and PMax, while Meta's spend is running ahead of its attributed return. Next: keep allocating by the weekly GA4 read, and rebalance Meta spend toward the specific ad sets and audiences GA4 confirms are converting, rather than broad demand generation.

Retention

Repeat-order flows are the reliable win

Automation recovered ₹1.11L at a high ₹18,588 order value with no manual effort, led by the C3 second-order pushes that bring happy customers back for a second purchase. Sample flows were quieter than July. Next: keep scaling the second-order flows, refresh the sample follow-up messaging, and stand up the new Sagepilot WhatsApp journeys for the festive period.

Funnel

Strong top and bottom, the middle is the opportunity

We are bringing in demand well (video and catalog reach) and closing high-value sales (4.94x India net return). The gap is the middle of the journey, turning samplers and browsers into buyers faster. Retention automations already do part of this. Next: connect the sample and abandoned-checkout journeys through Sagepilot so a customer who shows interest is gently guided to purchase rather than left to decide alone, especially through the festive sale.

How this adds up to our target.

The pieces are working: a healthy 4.94x India net return, a clear efficient channel in Google, reliable repeat-order retention, and strong Global order value. To reach ₹75L by October, the plan is realistic: concentrate spend on what GA4 proves is working, lift Meta's attributed return, and lean into the September festive sale with high-AOV catalogue tests and the new Sagepilot retention journeys. Growing these steadily, month on month, is what gets us there without over-relying on any single lever.
