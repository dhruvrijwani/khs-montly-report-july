01 · Executive Summary

Top Performance Overview

Business-as-usual month with strong India all-channel sales and GA4-led budget allocation.

₹58.72L in India all-channel sales, with paid media delivering a blended 5.10x ROAS.

Overall Executive Summary

June 2026 brought one urgent finding that overshadows the routine metrics: a subdomain of the India site (insitu.kalakaarihaath.com) is currently ranking for over 140 unrelated adult-content search terms, almost certainly a compromised or hijacked subdomain, not legitimate site content. This is flagged in full below and should be treated as the top priority for this week, not next month.

India completed 37 off-page actions and 3 blog posts of its own.

⚠

Urgent · Compromised subdomain detected (India)

This month's rank-tracking export (339 tracked keywords for kalakaarihaath.com) includes 143 entries, 42% of the total, ranking on the subdomain insitu.kalakaarihaath.com for search terms entirely unrelated to wall art or wallpaper. Several of these pages currently rank on page 1, meaning the subdomain is actively indexed and hosting unrelated adult/celebrity content, a strong signal of a hijacked or compromised subdomain.

This is likely what's driving the India Site Health score down to 77% (down 9 points this month). Left unresolved, it risks a Google manual action or algorithmic trust penalty against the whole kalakaarihaath.com domain, including the legitimate India site.

Immediate steps: (1) check DNS/CNAME records for the insitu subdomain to confirm control; (2) if compromised, remove the DNS record or reclaim the subdomain immediately; (3) request removal of the offending URLs via Google Search Console once resolved; (4) audit for any other unrecognised subdomains. This is the single top priority for July.
