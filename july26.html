01 · Executive Summary

Top Performance Overview

A build-and-scale month: new creatives, new geographies, and a sharper GA4-led budget engine.

₹52.37L in India all-channel sales at a 7.05x return, efficiency led the month.

July 2026 delivered ₹52.37L India all-channel sales across 455 orders, with the online store contributing ₹36.58L (₹33.34L ex-GST) from 403 orders on a controlled spend of ₹4,73,270, a strong 7.05x return. Budget was allocated on GA4 attribution, weighted week on week toward whichever platform showed the better return.

The Global store added $4,783.75 (₹3.11L net) across 11 orders at a 2.59x return, while new retargeting and lookalike campaigns went live internationally. On retention, the DelightChat WhatsApp engine recovered ₹1,67,296 from 12 orders, and a new RFM new-arrival broadcast re-engaged lapsed customers with fresh price points.

📈

GA4 keeps Google in the lead

On GA4, Google returned 5.55x (₹12.57L attributed) vs Meta's 1.09x (₹2.69L). These are GA4-attributed revenue, distinct from platform-reported numbers, and the basis on which budget stayed weighted toward Google.

🎯

New geographies tested

Tier-2 India targeting and specific global-market tests launched alongside Global retargeting and lookalike campaigns, expanding reach beyond the core base.

🧵

Wallpaper still leads the mix

62% of item revenue from Wallpaper; Upholstery held second at 11.7%. New creatives were tested across every category on both KH India and Global.

The figures below are GA4-attributed revenue, which is different from the revenue each platform reports in its own dashboard. We treat GA4 as the source of truth and allocate budget based on the GA4 report, reviewed week on week and shifted toward whichever platform GA4 shows performing better.

07 · What We Learned

Key Takeaways This Month

An honest read across products, channels, retention and the funnel, and what we will do about it.
Products

Wallpaper carries the business, samples open the door

Wallpaper is 62% of revenue but a smaller share of units, so it is our high-value core. By units sold, samples, upholstery and cushions lead, which tells us lower-ticket items are how new customers try the brand before committing to a full wallpaper order. New-arrival designs like Cypress Tales are already showing up in the top products. Next: keep pushing new arrivals and use samples deliberately as the first step into higher-value purchases.

Performance

Google is the efficient engine, Meta supports demand

On GA4, Google returned 5.55x versus Meta's 1.09x, so most of the budget rightly sat with Google. Meta still matters for reach and feeding fresh creative, but the attributed sales are coming from Google Search and PMax. Next: keep allocating by the weekly GA4 read, tighten Google targeting with the new audience signals, and work on lifting Meta's attributed return with better audience and creative combinations.

Retention

Automation is reliable, broadcast is untapped

Automations quietly recovered ₹1.61L at a high ₹17,839 order value with almost no manual effort. Broadcast reached a big audience but most messages went unread and few converted. The opportunity is clear: the audience and product are right, the message is not yet sharp enough. Next: rewrite broadcasts around the new arrival and its story, personalise the opening line, and test send times to lift read and conversion rates.

Funnel

Strong top and bottom, the middle is the opportunity

We are bringing in demand well (video and catalog reach) and closing high-value sales (7.05x India return). The gap is the middle of the journey, turning samplers and browsers into buyers faster. Retention automations already do part of this; broadcast and retargeting can do more. Next: connect the sample-buyer flows, WhatsApp follow-ups and retargeting so a customer who shows interest is gently guided to purchase rather than left to decide alone.

How this adds up to our target.

The pieces are working: a healthy 7.05x India return, a clear efficient channel in Google, reliable automation revenue, and early traction in new markets. To reach ₹75L by October, the plan is simple and realistic: scale spend on what GA4 proves is working, keep testing new creatives and new geographies, and fix the two soft spots, Meta's attributed return and the broadcast message. Growing these steadily, month on month, is what gets us there without over-relying on any single lever.

08 · What We Tested

July Experiments & Scaling Moves

The build-and-scale work behind July's numbers: new creatives, new geographies, and a broader retention play.
01

New creatives across every category

We tested fresh creatives for all product categories across KH India and Global: wallpaper, upholstery, cushions, wall art and decals, to find the next winners and keep the catalog and video pipeline fed. The new-arrival wallpaper catalog was the early breakout.

02

New geographies, Tier-2 India & global markets

New campaigns launched targeting Tier-2 locations in India and specific tested locations on the Global side, opening demand from newer areas beyond the core metros and existing international markets.

03

Global retargeting + lookalike launched

On the Global KH side we launched a retargeting campaign and a lookalike campaign, building the international funnel with warm-audience recovery and prospecting off our best converters.

04

Scaled India + new Google audience signals

We scaled spend across Meta and Google on KH India to push performance, and added new audience signals on Google Ads to sharpen PMax and Search targeting where GA4 shows the strongest attributed return.

05

RFM new-arrival broadcast (retention)

We launched broadcast messaging to the RFM audience for the new-arrival collection, reaching customers who had lost touch with the brand and re-engaging them with newly launched price-point products to bring them back into the buying cycle.

06

Messaging & tone experiments ahead

We will keep updating the broadcast messaging angle to improve performance, and test new messaging, communication patterns and style on retention, moving toward value and story-led framing to grow recovered revenue.
