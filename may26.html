01 — Executive Summary

Top Performance Overview

Business-as-usual month with disciplined spend and steady efficiency across both platforms.

₹40L in online store revenue, delivered at a blended 5.10x ROAS — in a pure BAU month.

May 2026 maintained strong momentum without any sale event. Paid channels generated ₹28.34L from 276 orders on a controlled spend of ₹5,55,384, while the combined India and Global online stores closed the month at ₹40L revenue.

The Global Shopify store contributed ₹4,52,151 (converted from $4,747 at ₹95.25/USD), validating early international traction. In parallel, a new retention program for sample-product buyers was launched to recapture customers who dropped off after their first purchase.

📈

5.69x ROAS on ₹2.65L spend — 155 orders and ₹15.07L revenue, driven by catalog and retargeting structures.

🔍

🎨

Catalog creatives dominated

🧵

Wallpaper remained the engine

Top 5 products were all wallpapers, led by Silk Route at ₹2.57L — confirming category-market fit.

07 — Road Ahead

Key Initiatives & Strategic Direction

Retention, scaling, and creative innovation for the months ahead.
— 01

Retention engine, activated

Activated WhatsApp automation on DelightChat for sample-product buyers — wallpaper, curtains, sheer curtains, upholstery, cushions and more — to recapture customers who dropped off from the website after sampling.

— 02

Retention roadmap ahead

Next, we are working on email automation for Global Kalakaari Haath, and will analyze the RFM report from Shopify to activate cohort-based WhatsApp flows for KH India.

— 03

Scale Google on proven signals

GA confirms both Brand Search and PMax performing strongly through the month. Additional budget will be deployed on Google campaigns to capture incremental high-intent demand at sustained efficiency.

— 04

Launch ads for new products

As new products are added to the website, we will launch dedicated ads for them to build early traction and accelerate discovery, while continuing to scale winning catalog and carousel structures from May.
