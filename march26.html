💡 Campaign Structure & Creative Performance

March 2026 was a strong full-month BAU operation across Meta, Google, and Pinterest. Catalog ads were the standout performers — particularly Best Sellers catalog on Meta, which consistently drove high-intent purchases. Creative formats including carousels, videos, and Instagram content creatives contributed to sustained engagement and conversion throughout the month. Daily budget optimization ensured spend was continuously reallocated towards the highest-performing campaigns, with non-performing ads paused proactively to protect efficiency. Google Ads delivered an exceptional 9.46x ROAS, while Meta dominated in order volume with 201 orders, making it the highest contributor to overall revenue.

🚀 Next Steps & Strategic Focus – April 2026

April Campaign Roadmap

Building on March's strong performance, April is split into two strategic phases:

  • April 1st – 15th | Summer Sale Period: Launch dedicated Summer Sale campaigns across Meta and Google Ads. Push higher budgets during this window to capitalise on seasonal purchase intent and drive elevated revenue. Creative focus will be on sale-specific messaging, urgency-led formats, and high-performing catalog structures carried forward from March.
  • April 16th – 30th | BAU Period: Transition out of sale mode — pause Summer Sale campaigns on Meta and Google, and launch post-sale BAU campaigns. Budgets will be shifted accordingly to maintain efficiency and sustain conversion momentum through the second half of the month.
